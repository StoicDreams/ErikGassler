## How I work with clients

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Working software early.** I deliver something usable as soon as possible, then improve it in small steps, so you see progress instead of status reports.
- **Tested and automated.** Changes come with automated tests and deploy through a pipeline, so the project keeps working after I hand it over.
- **Clear ownership.** I take responsibility for the work end to end, from architecture to deployment, and document what your team needs to carry it on.
